# EU AI Act Enforcement Timeline

The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) entered into force on 1 August 2024. Its obligations are phased in through 2030. This page lists every key compliance date and reflects the **Digital Omnibus on AI Regulation** package, on which the Council and the European Parliament reached a **provisional political agreement on 7 May 2026** (Council adopted its position on 13 March 2026; Parliament adopted its on 26 March 2026; the first trilogue on 28 April 2026 ended without agreement; the agreement landed in the second round on 7 May).

> **⚖️ Status caveat (8 May 2026):** The 2 December 2026, 2 December 2027, 2 August 2027 and 2 August 2028 dates below reflect the Digital Omnibus on AI provisional agreement reached by the co-legislators on 7 May 2026. The text must still be **endorsed by the Council and the European Parliament**, then pass legal-linguistic revision before being **published in the Official Journal of the EU**. **Until OJ publication, the original Regulation 2024/1689 dates remain legally binding** — most importantly 2 August 2026 for Annex III high-risk obligations, Article 50 transparency, and the Article 57 sandbox deadline. Plan for the earlier of the two timelines.

```
     │

     │

     │         Article 50 transparency, and Article 57 sandboxes

     │

     │         Legacy GPAI models must comply
     │

     │

     │

```

## Detailed Date Reference

### 2024

| Date | Obligation | Who Is Affected |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **1 August 2024** | EU AI Act enters into force (published in OJ 12 July 2024) | All |

### 2025 ✅ (in force)

| Date | Obligation | Who Is Affected |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **2 February 2025** | Prohibitions on unacceptable-risk AI practices take effect (Article 5). Penalties for prohibited practices are enforceable. | All providers and deployers |
| **2 February 2025** | AI literacy obligation takes effect (Article 4). Organisations must ensure staff dealing with AI systems have sufficient AI literacy. | All providers and deployers |
| **2 August 2025** | GPAI model obligations take effect (Articles 51–55). Providers of GPAI models must comply with transparency, copyright, and (where applicable) systemic risk requirements. | GPAI model providers |
| **2 August 2025** | Member States must designate national market surveillance authorities and notifying authorities. | Member States |
| **2 August 2025** | The EU AI Board becomes operational. | EU institutions |
| **2 August 2025** | The European AI Office's enforcement powers for GPAI begin to ramp up. Information requests, voluntary recommendations, and model evaluations are available; binding fines for GPAI providers begin **2 August 2026** (one year after rules take effect). | GPAI model providers |
| **10 July 2025** | GPAI Code of Practice final version published; adherence provides a rebuttable presumption of compliance. | GPAI model providers |
| **6 October 2025** | The proposed AI Liability Directive (AILD, COM/2022/496) is **formally withdrawn** by the European Commission (notice of withdrawal published in OJ). The EU AI liability framework is now two-pillar: revised PLD plus national tort law. See [AI Liability in the EU](AI-Liability-in-the-EU). | All |
| **10 October 2025** | **Italy's Legge 132/2025** (the first national AI law in the EU) enters into force. See [National Implementation by Member States](National-Implementation). | Italian providers, deployers and public bodies |

### 2026

| Date | Obligation | Who Is Affected |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **2 February 2026** ✅ | European Commission guidelines on the practical implementation of the high-risk classification (Article 6(5)) and on prohibited practices. | European Commission |
| **20 January 2026** ✅ | EDPB-EDPS Joint Opinion 1/2026 on the Digital Omnibus on AI is published, raising fundamental-rights concerns about several simplification measures. | All |
| **15 April 2026** ✅ | EDPB Draft Guidelines 1/2026 on the processing of personal data for scientific research are published (public consultation open until 25 June 2026). | Controllers using AI training data, research bodies |
| **7 May 2026** ✅ | Council and Parliament reach **provisional agreement** on the Digital Omnibus on AI (Omnibus VII package). Endorsement by Council and Parliament, then OJ publication, are pending. | All |
| **2 August 2026** *(pre-Omnibus, legally binding until OJ publication)* | Annex III high-risk obligations, Article 50 transparency obligations, and the Article 57 sandbox deadline take full effect under the original Regulation 2024/1689. **Each of these dates is shifted by the Omnibus** once published; until then they bind. | Providers and deployers; Member States |
| **2 December 2026** *(Omnibus, provisional 7 May 2026)* | Article 50 transparency obligations for limited-risk systems take full effect, including AI-generated content watermarking requirements. The grace period was reduced from 6 months to **3 months** under the 7 May agreement. | Providers and deployers |
| **9 December 2026** | Transposition deadline for the revised Product Liability Directive (Directive 2024/2853) into national law. | Member States, all market participants |

### 2027

| Date | Obligation | Who Is Affected |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **2 August 2027** *(Omnibus, provisional 7 May 2026)* | National AI regulatory sandboxes must be operational under Article 57. The 7 May agreement **postponed this deadline by one year** (it was originally 2 August 2026). | Member States |
| **2 August 2027** | GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must be brought into compliance. | GPAI model providers (legacy models) |
| **2 December 2027** *(Omnibus, provisional 7 May 2026)* | **Main enforcement date for high-risk Annex III systems.** Articles 9–15 requirements take full effect. EU AI Database registration (Art. 49), FRIA (Art. 27), post-market monitoring and serious-incident reporting (Arts. 72–73) apply. Full penalty regime active: up to €35M / 7% (prohibited), €15M / 3% (high-risk), €7.5M / 1% (incorrect information). | Providers and deployers of high-risk AI |

### 2028

| Date | Obligation | Who Is Affected |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **2 August 2028** *(Omnibus, provisional 7 May 2026)* | High-risk AI systems embedded in regulated products (Annex I / Article 6(1)) — medical devices, machinery, civil aviation, vehicles, etc. — must comply. | Providers of regulated-product AI |

### 2030

| Date | Obligation | Who Is Affected |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **2 August 2030** | Legacy public-sector AI systems in use before 2 August 2026 must comply. | Public-sector deployers |
| **31 December 2030** | Large-scale IT system AI components (Annex IX — SIS, VIS, EES, ETIAS, Eurodac) must comply. | EU institutions / MS agencies |

## The Digital Omnibus on AI Regulation

In November 2025 the European Commission published the **Digital Omnibus on AI Regulation** proposal, designed to rebalance the enforcement calendar in light of delayed harmonised standards and incomplete national implementation, and to add a new Article 5 prohibition.

**Key changes** (as reflected in this timeline, subject to formal adoption and OJ publication):

- **Annex III high-risk obligations** moved from 2 August 2026 to **2 December 2027** (long-stop date; sooner if harmonised standards are published earlier).
- **Regulated-product high-risk** (Annex I / Art. 6(1)) moved to **2 August 2028**.
- **Article 50 transparency** obligations moved to **2 December 2026**, with the grace period reduced from 6 months to 3 months in the 7 May 2026 trilogue agreement.
- **Article 57 sandbox deadline** moved from 2 August 2026 to **2 August 2027** (postponed by one year under the 7 May 2026 agreement).
- **New ninth Article 5 prohibition** added: AI used to generate non-consensual sexual or intimate content depicting identifiable persons, and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Same penalty tier as the other Article 5 prohibitions (up to €35M / 7% of global turnover). See [Prohibited AI Practices](Prohibited-AI-Practices).

**Legislative milestones:**

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 19 November 2025 | Commission tables the Digital Omnibus on AI proposal as part of the broader Digital Omnibus package. |
| 13 March 2026 | Council general approach adopted. |
| 18–19 March 2026 | EP IMCO / LIBE committees jointly adopt their report (101–9–8). |
| 26 March 2026 | EP plenary mandate confirmed; trilogue opened. |
| 28 April 2026 | First political trilogue ends without agreement after roughly 12 hours of negotiation. |
| **7 May 2026** | **Council and Parliament reach a provisional agreement** (Omnibus VII package). |
| Pending | Endorsement by Council and Parliament, legal-linguistic revision, publication in the Official Journal. |

**Until OJ publication, the 2 August 2026 deadlines in the original Regulation 2024/1689 remain legally binding.** Organisations should continue to plan for the earlier of the two dates.

## Practical Implications

- **Already in force:** AI literacy and prohibited-practice compliance (Feb 2025); GPAI provider obligations (Aug 2025); Italian Legge 132/2025 (Oct 2025).
- **Most urgent upcoming deadlines:** Article 50 transparency (2 December 2026 if Omnibus is published in time, otherwise 2 August 2026); PLD transposition (9 December 2026); Italy's implementing decrees under Law 132/2025 (10 October 2026).
- **Main compliance target:** 2 December 2027 (Omnibus) or 2 August 2026 (pre-Omnibus) — whichever survives endorsement and publication.
